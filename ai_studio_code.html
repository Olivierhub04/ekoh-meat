Your Challenges

Do any of these situations sound familiar?

Every day, hospitality groups, retailers, and food manufacturers lose margin to unreliable meat supply. If any of these obstacles resonate, ekoh-meat is your production partner.

Quality & Shelf Life

Cold-Chain Breaks & Early Spoilage

Temperature fluctuations during transport shorten shelf life, trigger shrinkage, and put food safety reputations at risk.

  • Lack of real-time temperature logs upon delivery
  • High purge loss and inconsistent vacuum sealing
  • Unpredictable bacterial counts in warm seasons
Supply Continuity

Stockouts & Seasonal Price Spikes

Relying on fragmented small-scale intermediaries leaves your menus and supermarket shelves exposed during peak demand.

  • Missed deliveries before weekends and holidays
  • Inability to scale volumes for large promotions
  • Inconsistent carcass grading week to week
Compliance

Missing Traceability & Sanitary Audits

Modern corporate buyers and regulatory authorities demand full origin records, health certificates, and batch accountability.

  • No documented farm origin or veterinary clearance
  • Manual paperwork slowing down receiving docks
  • Non-compliance with international hospitality standards
Kitchen Efficiency

Unstandardized Cuts & Trim Waste

Receiving irregular whole carcasses forces your kitchen or butcher staff to spend costly labor trimming excess bone and fat.

  • Inconsistent steak weights and portion yields
  • High kitchen prep labor and offcut waste
  • Lack of ready-to-cook marinated or minced specs
